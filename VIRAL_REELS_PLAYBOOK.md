## THE 5 NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

Every viral Reel must have ALL five of these. Missing even one cuts virality by 50%+.

### Rule 1: The 1-Second Hook
The first frame must answer: **"Why should I stop scrolling?"**

| Hook Type | Description | When to Use | Example |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Pattern Interrupt** | Something visually unexpected that breaks the feed pattern | Comedy, stunts | A person mid-fall, an impossible object |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Text or visual that creates an information gap | Educational, reveals | "Nobody talks about this..." |
| **Direct Address** | Face looking at camera, mid-sentence, animated expression | Advice, motivation | Close-up face with text hook overlay |
| **Transformation Tease** | Show the "after" first, then the "before" | DIY, fitness, cooking | Stunning final dish, then "here's how" |
| **Mid-Action Start** | Video begins in the middle of the action, not the setup | Sports, cooking, stunts | Already flipping, already pouring |
| **Shock Value** | Something that triggers immediate "wait what?" | Comedy, reactions | Unexpected juxtaposition |
| **Text Hook** | Bold text on screen that demands reading | Any niche | "This $3 trick changed everything" |

**CRITICAL**: The first frame must contain EITHER a face with eye contact OR bold text. Never start with an empty landscape, static object, or back-of-head shot.

### Rule 2: Face Forward
Videos with faces average **6.8/10 virality** vs **3.5/10 for faceless** content.

- **Face centered in frame** for the first 3 seconds minimum
- **Direct eye contact with camera** — triggers parasocial brain circuitry
- **Animated expression** — wide eyes, open mouth, hand gestures
- **Face fills at least 40% of frame** in the opening shot
- Exception: food/ASMR content can work faceless IF the visual is inherently satisfying

### Rule 3: Complete Narrative Arc
Every Reel needs a beginning, middle, and end — even in 7 seconds.

| Arc Type | Structure | Best For | Duration |
|----------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Setup → Punchline** | Build expectation, subvert it | Comedy | 7-15s |
| **Problem → Solution** | Show the pain, deliver the fix | Tutorial, life hack | 15-30s |
| **Before → After** | Show the transformation | DIY, fitness, cooking | 10-20s |
| **Tension → Release** | Build suspense, deliver resolution | Stunts, reveals | 10-30s |
| **Curiosity → Reveal** | Tease what's coming, show it | Any niche | 7-15s |

**NEVER** make a Reel that is just "a pretty thing existing." Aesthetic-only content averages **3.5/10** virality.

### Rule 4: Psychological Trigger
Every Reel must activate at least ONE of these emotional responses:

| Trigger | Description | Share Behavior | Best Niches |
|---------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Humor** | Makes viewer laugh or smile | "You HAVE to see this" shares | Comedy, reactions |
| **Relatability** | Viewer thinks "that's literally me" | Tag-your-friend shares | Couple, lifestyle, work |
| **Curiosity** | Viewer needs to see what happens | Watch-to-end retention | Reveals, tutorials |
| **Awe/Wow** | Viewer is genuinely impressed | "How did they do that?" shares | Stunts, nature, art |
| **Inspiration** | Viewer feels motivated | "I needed this today" saves | Fitness, motivation |
| **Satisfaction** | Viewer feels pleasant completion | Replay behavior | ASMR, cooking, cleaning |

### Rule 5: Engagement CTA
Every Reel must drive at least one engagement action:

- **Share trigger**: "Tag someone who needs to see this" / "Send this to your..."
- **Save trigger**: Provide reference value (recipe, checklist, tip list)
- **Comment trigger**: Ask a question, create debate, use fill-in-the-blank
- **Replay trigger**: Add hidden details, seamless loops, fast text

## THE 10 PROVEN VIRAL FORMATS

Based on analysis of creators who went from 0 to millions of views organically.

### Format 1: The Talking Head (Virality: 8/10)
**Why it works**: Face + eye contact + speech = strongest parasocial trigger

```
STRUCTURE:
- Frame 1: Face close-up, mid-sentence, with bold text hook overlay
- Editing: Jump cuts every 1.5-2 seconds (removes dead air)
- Lighting: Even front light, clean background (solid color or blurred)
- Pacing: FAST — dense information, no pauses
- Audio: Original voice with captions (85% of users start on mute)
- Duration: 15-60 seconds
- CTA: "Save this for later" or controversial question in caption

VISUAL SPEC FOR AI GENERATION:
- 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- Person centered, head-and-shoulders framing
- Animated facial expressions (wide eyes, hand gestures)
- Clean solid-color or gradient background
- Bold sans-serif text overlay at top third
```

### Format 2: The Relatable Couple/Friend Skit (Virality: 8/10)
**Why it works**: Universal relatability + tag-your-partner = comment multiplier

```
STRUCTURE:
- Frame 1: Two people in a recognizable scenario (bed, car, kitchen)
- Narrative: Mundane situation → twist/punchline OR sweet moment
- Lighting: Warm, practical (bedroom lamps, kitchen light)
- Pacing: Medium — let the scenario breathe, then payoff
- Audio: Trending sound or original dialogue
- Duration: 10-20 seconds
- CTA: "Tag your person" / "This is literally us right?"

VISUAL SPEC FOR AI GENERATION:
- 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- Warm color palette (amber, cream, soft lighting)
- Indoor domestic setting (bedroom, kitchen, couch)
- Two people with visible facial expressions
- Natural body language, not staged-looking
```

### Format 3: The Silent Cooking/ASMR (Virality: 7/10)
**Why it works**: Satisfying visuals + sound triggers completion drive. Emily Mariko's silent salmon bowl got 57M views.

```
STRUCTURE:
- Frame 1: Close-up of ingredients or action mid-pour
- Narrative: Ingredient assembly → cooking process → final reveal
- Lighting: Bright, clean, overhead or 45-degree angle
- Pacing: Medium — each step gets 2-3 seconds
- Audio: ASMR sounds (sizzling, chopping, pouring) or lo-fi music
- Duration: 15-30 seconds
- CTA: "Save this recipe" / "Would you try this?"

VISUAL SPEC FOR AI GENERATION:
- 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- Overhead or 45-degree close-up angles
- Bright, clean kitchen environment
- Vivid food colors (greens, reds, golden browns)
- Smooth, steady camera — no shake

```

### Format 4: The Transformation/Before-After (Virality: 8/10)

```
STRUCTURE:
- Frame 1: Show the "AFTER" first (the stunning result) for 1 second
- Frame 2: Cut to "BEFORE" with text "Here's how..."
- Middle: Fast-paced process montage
- End: Slow reveal of the finished transformation
- Lighting: Consistent between before/after to maximize contrast
- Duration: 15-30 seconds
- CTA: "Save for when you try this"

VISUAL SPEC FOR AI GENERATION:
- 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- Split: first frame is polished/beautiful, then cut to raw/messy
- Speed ramps: 2-4x during process, normal for key moments
- Final reveal at normal speed with slight slow-motion
- Bold text overlays marking "BEFORE" and "AFTER"
```

VISUAL SPEC FOR AI GENERATION:

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR AI VIDEO GENERATION

### Resolution & Format
```
Width: 1080px
Height: 1920px
Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (vertical/portrait)
FPS: 30fps (minimum) or 60fps (premium)
Duration: 7-30 seconds (sweet spot: 12-18 seconds)
Format: MP4, H.264 codec
Max file size: 100MB
```

### Color & Lighting
```
Warm palette preferred (engagement data shows warm tones outperform cool)
High saturation for food, nature, lifestyle content
Clean whites for tutorial/educational content
Golden-hour lighting for motivational/cinematic content
High contrast between subject and background
Avoid flat, grey, desaturated imagery
```

### Text Overlay Specifications
```
Font: Bold sans-serif (Impact, Helvetica Bold, or similar)
Size: Large enough to read on a phone (minimum 48pt equivalent)
Position: Top third of frame for hook text, center for emphasis
Color: White with black stroke/shadow for visibility over any background
Animation: Appear on beat, word-by-word highlight for captions
Maximum words per screen: 6-8 (must be readable in <1 second)
```

### Pacing Guidelines
```
Average shot duration: 1.5-3 seconds (fast-paced)
Scene transitions: Cut on beats or natural action points
Speed ramps: 2x for process/filler, 0.5x for key moments/reveals
Jump cuts: Every 1.5-2 seconds for talking head
Never hold a static shot for more than 3 seconds
```

## PSYCHOLOGICAL MECHANICS REFERENCE

### The Virality Equation
```
Virality = (Hook Strength x Emotional Trigger x Completion Rate x Share Impulse)
           ÷ Scroll Friction

Where:
- Hook Strength: Does the first frame stop the thumb? (1-10)
- Emotional Trigger: Does it make them FEEL something? (1-10)
- Completion Rate: Do they watch to the end? (0-100%)
- Share Impulse: Do they want to send this to someone? (1-10)
- Scroll Friction: How much effort to understand it? (1-10, lower = better)
```

### Cognitive Biases to Exploit
| Bias | How to Use | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Curiosity Gap** | Tease information, delay the reveal | "The #1 mistake everyone makes..." |
| **Completion Drive** | Start a process that viewers need to see finished | Showing 90% of a drawing, cut before done |
| **Social Proof** | Show numbers, crowds, reactions | "10M people tried this..." |
| **FOMO** | Imply exclusivity or time-sensitivity | "Before this gets taken down..." |
| **Loss Aversion** | Frame as avoiding a negative | "Stop doing THIS immediately" |
| **Bandwagon** | Show widespread adoption | "Everyone's making this recipe now" |
| **Authority** | Position as expert/insider | "As a chef of 15 years..." |
| **Reciprocity** | Give free value upfront | Free tutorial → follow CTA |

### The 4 Share Triggers (Why People DM Reels)
1. **Identity Signal**: "This is SO me" — they share to tell others who they are
2. **Utility**: "You need to try this" — they share because it's useful
3. **Emotional Resonance**: "This made me feel X" — they share the feeling
4. **Social Currency**: "Look what I found" — they share to seem cool/informed

### The 3 Save Triggers (Why People Bookmark)
1. **Reference**: "I'll need this later" — tutorials, recipes, checklists
2. **Aspiration**: "I want this life/body/skill" — fitness, travel, fashion
3. **Aesthetic**: "I want my feed to look like this" — mood boards, design inspo

## NICHE-SPECIFIC VIRAL FORMULAS

### Food/Cooking
- **Hero format**: 3-step recipe in under 30 seconds
- **Secret weapon**: Overhead angle + speed ramps + final steam shot
- **Audio**: ASMR sizzle sounds outperform music by 2x
- **Text**: "Save this recipe" drives highest save rate of any CTA

### Fitness
- **Hero format**: "Then vs Now" transformation with timeline
- **Secret weapon**: Side-by-side comparison + specific numbers ("Lost 30lbs in 6 months")
- **Audio**: Motivational speech + beat drop at transformation reveal
- **Text**: Specific numbers > vague claims ("Day 1 vs Day 365" > "My fitness journey")

### Comedy
- **Hero format**: Setup-punchline in under 15 seconds
- **Secret weapon**: Relatable scenario + unexpected twist
- **Audio**: Trending sounds drive 2-3x more reach
- **Text**: Minimal — comedy should work visually

### Travel
- **Hero format**: "Must-do activity in [city]" save-worthy guide
- **Secret weapon**: Numbered list format (saves + shares)
- **Audio**: Trending wanderlust audio
- **Text**: Location name prominent + numbered steps

### Business/Education
- **Hero format**: Talking head with controversial opening + 3 actionable tips
- **Secret weapon**: Authority framing ("After 10 years in marketing...")
- **Audio**: Original voice with word-by-word highlighted captions
- **Text**: Hook text in frame 1, captions throughout, "Save this" at end
